## National Strategic Programs

### MEXT-Supported Embedded Systems Education
**Program**: "Human Resource Development Hub for Information Technology Practitioners in Growth Fields - Embedded Systems Track"  
**Period**: 2016–present (ongoing)  
**Role**: Core faculty contributor  
**Focus**: 
- PBL-based embedded systems curriculum development
- Industry-university collaboration (30+ partner companies)
- Human resource cultivation for information technology

### Semiconductor Talent Development Initiative (2025–2026)

**Prefecture-Level Program**  
"Ehime Semiconductor Industry Network" coordinated by Ehime Prefecture Governor  
- **半導体集積回路におけるテスト技術およびセキュリティ技術講座**  
- Capacity: ~30 professionals/year
- Focus: Advanced semiconductor IC testing and security

**National Multi-University Program**  
"Chushikoku VISTA (Value-Integrated Semiconductor Talent Acceleration)"  
- **Period**: 2026–present (newly launched)
- **Funding**: MEXT "University Education Revitalization Strategy Promotion Expenses - Semiconductor Human Resource Development Hub Formation Project"
- **Consortium**: Hiroshima University, Okayama University, Yamaguchi University, Ehime University
- **Target**: ~100 students/year (bachelor to master level, regional coverage)
- **Courses Led**: Semiconductor fundamentals, IC test technology, advanced security mechanisms

## International Collaborative Research

### Japan-China Bilateral Exchange Research
**Partner Organization**: Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) & China National Natural Science Foundation (NSFC)  
**Focus**: Chiplet Systems Lifecycle Reliability  
**Participants**:
- Japanese institutions: 2 universities + industry partners
- Chinese institutions: 2 universities + research centers
- **Research Areas**:
  - 3D integration and chiplet design
  - Quality monitoring and repair mechanisms
  - Secure testing and authentication
  - Thermal-aware layout optimization

**Multi-university and cross-regional collaborations** on:
- Advanced IC test methodologies

- AI-chip reliability and optimization

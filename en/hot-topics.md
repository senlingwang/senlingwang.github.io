## Research goal

Industrial geometry, equipment, and tasks change over time. We connect observations from real sites to a shared environment model, then use that model for simulation, robot planning, and physical execution. The goal is a system that can adapt as conditions change.

## Four research pillars

<div class="hot-topic-grid">
  <div class="hot-topic-card"><h3>1. Real-to-Sim</h3><p>Reconstruct equipment and spaces from photographs and point clouds to create 3D scenes for robot experiments.</p></div>
  <div class="hot-topic-card"><h3>2. AI-agent coordination</h3><p>A Prime Agent decomposes and assigns tasks; Slave Agents connect plans to navigation, sensing, and manipulation tools.</p></div>
  <div class="hot-topic-card"><h3>3. Sim-to-Real</h3><p>Progressively connect simulation plans with mapping, navigation, and inspection on physical robots.</p></div>
  <div class="hot-topic-card"><h3>4. Physical-virtual interaction</h3><p>Return field observations to the digital twin and work toward replanning from object states and histories.</p></div>
</div>

## Current work and next milestone

With our laboratory-developed AI-agent robot HIME and related systems, we are testing selected navigation, observation, and action-generation capabilities in the field. Current work focuses on completing a reliable inspection cycle, updating the environment model as sites change, and reusing acquired skills in later tasks.

Visit the [project site](https://ehimetakahashilab.github.io/Computer-System-Lab.io/index.html) for experiments, research outputs, and videos. See also [research areas](/en/research/#physicalai) and [joint research](/en/projects/).

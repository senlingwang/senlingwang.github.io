## Teaching Philosophy
I am committed to **practice-based learning** that connects academic theory with real-world applications. My teaching emphasizes **system-level thinking**, **hands-on experimentation**, and **collaboration with industry partners**. I aim to prepare students not only with technical knowledge but also with **communication skills**, **project management abilities**, and **ethical awareness** for careers in engineering and research.
